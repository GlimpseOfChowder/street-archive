Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

Street Archive is a free, community-run map of car clubs ("crews"), meets, cruises and car shows in the Philippines. This page explains what information the site handles and why.

Browsing the map

You can view the map, crews and meets without an account. We don't use analytics or advertising trackers.

Signing in with Google

To post a meet or add a crew you sign in with Google. Google shares with us only your name, email address, profile picture and Google account ID. We use these to:

  • link your posts to your account so we can review them and prevent spam and fake listings;
  • show you the status of your own posts ("My posts");
  • contact you about a post if needed.

Your email address is never shown publicly on the map. We don't sell, rent or share your information with anyone, and we don't use it for advertising.

What you post

Meets and crews you submit (name, place, time, description, links, logo link and the map location you pick) are reviewed by the site admin and, once approved, are public on the map. The optional "Your FB page" contact field is seen only by the admin. Don't include private information in public fields.

Where data is stored

Submissions and account details are stored in a Google Sheet owned by the site admin, using Google's services. Only the admin can access it.

Stored in your browser

The site keeps a few things in your own browser (local storage): your sign-in session, meet reminders you set, your map style, which meets you've already seen, and a cached copy of the public map data. Clearing your browser data removes them.

Your location

"Show my location" and "Near me" use your device's location only inside your browser to centre the map and sort meets by distance. It is never sent to us or stored.

Third-party services

  • Google — sign-in, and Google Sheets / Apps Script for storing submissions.
  • Map tiles — CARTO, Esri and OpenStreetMap supply the map images; your browser requests them directly from those providers.
  • Place search — OpenStreetMap Nominatim receives the text you search for.
  • Links — crew and event links open external sites (e.g. Facebook), which have their own privacy policies.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your posts and account details at any time by emailing dafuq.ruz@gmail.com from the email you signed in with. You can also remove Street Archive's access from your Google Account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Children

Street Archive is not intended for children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date above.
